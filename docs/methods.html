Model and sampling

  • Model. One-stage random-effects model on the log-OR with a hybrid likelihood: exact binomial for count trials and a normal likelihood for REMAP-CAP, which enters only as an adjusted contrast. Under decision D2a (30 October 2026, extended to F3 and F4 on 10 November) the short-term sets F1, F3 and F4 use its ICU-discharge contrast (log-OR 0.399, SE 0.487), the earliest adjusted mortality contrast it reports, and the 90-day set F2 uses its day-90 pooled contrast (log-OR 0.445, SE 0.362). Both SEs are derived from the published 95% credible intervals pending the investigators’ values; F1 is refitted with each SE varied (ICU discharge 0.40 and 0.60; day 90 0.30 and 0.45). Trial baselines are unpooled fixed effects with an N(0, 10) prior. Implemented in Stan (stan/hybrid_ma.stan) via cmdstanr; the protocol text names brms, so this is a documented deviation.
  • Fits. F1 primary short-term (7 trials), F2 90-day (3), F3 broad-severe (10), F4 all-hospitalised (17); F1 with an agent hierarchy; prior ladder; tipping-point analyses; sensitivity fits; meta-regressions.
  • Sampling. 4 chains × 2000 post-warmup draws, adapt_delta 0.99 (raised automatically to 0.999, then 0.9999, if a fit has divergences; the convergence page lists any fit that needed this), max_treedepth 12, seed 7291. A fit is accepted only if R-hat < 1.01, bulk and tail ESS > 400 and there are zero divergences.
  • Priors. Pooled log-OR μ: Normal(0, 1) in the main fits (the reference rung), with the alternatives on the prior ladder page; between-trial SD τ ~ half-Normal(0, 0.5); between-agent SD ω ~ half-Normal(0, 0.25); trial baselines Normal(0, 10). The sensitivity page varies τ, ω and the baseline prior.
  • Quantities. Posterior median OR with a 95% quantile credible interval (CrI; HDI columns are in the downloadable tables), P(OR<1), P(OR<0.9), P(OR<0.833), P(OR>1.1), τ, the predictive OR for a new trial, and the absolute risk reduction (ARR) and number needed to treat (NNT). OR < 0.833 (= 1/1.2) is an odds ratio for survival of at least 1.2, that is a reduction of about 17% in the odds of death; it is the threshold REMAP-CAP used in its futility rule.

Risk of bias

Risk of bias was assessed with the Cochrane RoB 2 tool for all-cause mortality at each trial’s primary-analysis timepoint (effect of assignment), across the five RoB 2 domains, with the overall rating derived by the RoB 2 rule. For subgroup-derived trials, domain 5 also records whether the subgroup was pre-specified. The seven primary-set trials were rated independently by two assessors (assessor 1, an AI-generated draft from the extracted signalling information; assessor 2, a human reviewer working from the full texts and signalling questions). Disagreements were listed in a reconciliation sheet with a proposal for each, and the consensus was recorded by the project lead adopting those proposals; the second assessor’s confirmation is pending. Sensitivity-set trials have only the assessor-1 draft. The ratings feed the low-risk-of-bias sensitivity analysis (S-ii), which is run for each assessor and for the consensus. Details, agreement and the consensus rationale are on the risk of bias page.
