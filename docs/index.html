Draft analysis of adjunctive systemic corticosteroids versus placebo or no corticosteroid in adults with severe community-acquired pneumonia (sCAP): a Bayesian random-effects meta-analysis of all-cause mortality on the odds-ratio scale, with an agent-level hierarchy, a prior ladder and tipping-point analyses. The review is not yet registered, and several decisions below are awaiting confirmation by the project lead.

kable(dec, caption = "D1 and D2: default and alternatives (F1).")
  • D1 (ESCAPe short-term row). Default is in-hospital (protocol field 24: earliest of in-hospital, 28-day, 30-day), which leans toward harm. The project status log lists the trial’s d60 row, so the default and the log differ. The pooled result moves from P(OR<1) = 0.95 (default) to 0.97 (d60) and 0.97 (IPD 30-day).
  • D2 (REMAP-CAP in the short-term fit). There is no valid short-term contrast; the d90 pooled contrast is used by default. Excluding REMAP-CAP gives P(OR<1) = 0.98. The contrast SE is interim (0.362, from the credible interval), and the result is sensitive to it (SE 0.30 to 0.45: P(OR<1) 0.94 to 0.96).
  • D3 (prior ladder). The enthusiastic N(log 0.70, 0.20), reference N(0, 1.0) and skeptical N(0, 0.35) rungs are proposals; the mild-harm N(log 1.10, 0.20) and REMAP-CAP-harm N(log 1.50, 0.35) rungs are protocol-fixed. Sign-off needed on centre and SD of the first three.
  • D4 (τ and ω hyperpriors). Primary τ ~ half-Normal(0, 0.5) and ω ~ half-Normal(0, 0.25) are proposals; the sensitivity grid is reported below.

Choices not on the D1–D4 list that also need confirmation

  • Meta-regression coefficient prior b ~ Normal(0, 1), applied to covariates centred and (for continuous ones) scaled to SD 1.
  • Pooled-baseline comparison model: a0 ~ Normal(0, 2), sigma_a ~ half-Normal(0, 1) (comparison only; primary baselines stay unpooled).
  • Coding of binary timing_le24h: 1 if the stated maximum window to first dose is ≤24 h, 0 if the stated maximum or median is longer; coding table in the meta-regression section. Torres (within 36 h) and SONIA (within 48 h) are coded 0.
  • S-IPD: the analysis plan lists Snijders and Santeon among F4 IPD replacements, but analysis_mortality.csv has no IPD row for either, so only the trials that have one are swapped (F1: Confalonieri, Torres, ESCAPe, CAPE COD; F4 adds Meijvis and STEP).

At a glance
