## Field 26. Data extraction (selection and coding)

*Selection.* Records were de-duplicated (DOI, PMID and normalised title) and screened by title/abstract against the protocol criteria in one pass by a single AI-assisted screener: rule-based pre-classification of every record, manual reading of every candidate's title and abstract, and a title scan of all rule-excluded records. The screening log records the decision, reason code and basis for each record. This departs from the protocol's independent duplicate screening. A second independent human screener will review all retained records (135) and a random 10% sample of the exclusions; disagreements will be resolved by discussion or a third reviewer, and any resulting change in the included set will be reported. Full texts were assessed by RL, with MCA as second reviewer, and the process is reported in a PRISMA 2020 flow diagram. Reports sought but not obtained are listed in the flow diagram and the repository.

*Extraction.* A first extraction pass into a locked template (study identifiers, design, setting, severity definition, population, agent, dose converted to prednisone-equivalents, timing, duration, arm-level mortality events and denominators at each reported timepoint, and for adjusted or adaptive trials the adjusted log-OR with its standard error and model) was done by a single AI-assisted extractor on 22 October 2026; every value carries its source record, page, location and a verbatim quote. A second extractor (human) then checked the numeric rows against the source PDFs (27 October) and returned a row-by-row verification of the analysis rows (31 October); verification is recorded in the database and discrepancies are reconciled against the source and logged. This departs from the protocol's two independent extractors. For REMAP-CAP and any adaptive or adjusted trial the adjusted contrast and its standard error are extracted; crude arm counts are recorded but not used as the input for that trial. Investigator-supplied adjusted estimates were not obtained; the REMAP-CAP standard errors are derived from the published credible intervals (see the change log, item 4).

## Field 27. Risk of bias (quality) assessment

Risk of bias is assessed with the Cochrane RoB 2 tool for the mortality outcome (effect of assignment) at each trial's primary-analysis timepoint, across the five domains, with an overall judgement per trial. For the 7 trials of the primary set, two assessors worked independently: assessor 1 produced an AI-generated draft from the signalling information extracted for each trial, and assessor 2 (human) rated from the full texts and signalling questions. Disagreements were listed in a reconciliation sheet; a consensus was recorded by the lead on 4 November 2026 and amended on 10 November 2026 to follow the second assessor's judgements, so that it now equals assessor 2's ratings. For the 11 trials of the sensitivity set only the assessor-1 draft exists; a second assessment is planned and these ratings are not used in the sensitivity analysis restricted to low-risk-of-bias trials, which covers the primary set. Open-label trials are flagged for performance and detection bias on subjective secondary outcomes, although all-cause mortality is objective. Small and heterogeneously reported trials are checked for indexing, severe-CAP eligibility and reporting completeness. Risk-of-bias judgements inform the pre-specified sensitivity analysis restricted to low-risk trials (run for each assessor and for the consensus) and feed the GRADE assessment.
