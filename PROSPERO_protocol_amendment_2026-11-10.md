## Detailed version (longer; kept for the decision log, not for the form)

**Changes and deviations from protocol v2 (16 October 2026), recorded 10 November 2026, before registration.**
*(1) Searches.* Ovid MEDLINE, PubMed, Embase and CENTRAL were searched and exported on 19 and 20 October 2026 (export file dates; the exact search dates were not recorded). ClinicalTrials.gov and the WHO ICTRP were not searched directly; registered and ongoing trials were identified from CENTRAL records, which index registry entries, and by hand-searching the inclusion lists of recent reviews and guidelines. The Ovid MEDLINE primary set (strategy line 28) was not exported, and the PubMed broader-indication set (#5) returned 10 records; the same content was covered by the other databases (the pre-specified recall check was passed, with all 19 named records retrieved, and no retained record depended on Ovid MEDLINE or PubMed alone). The search will be re-run before the final analysis.
*(2) Sensitivity fit (ix), added 27 October 2026.* A fit that excludes trials with less than 48 hours of corticosteroid treatment (only Marik 1993, a single bolus) was added to the pre-specified sensitivity analyses, after data extraction (completed 22 October) and before the first analysis run (29 October).
*(3) SONIA agent-mix rule, added 27 October 2026.* SONIA 2025 (all-hospitalised, sensitivity set) gives several corticosteroid agents by a second randomisation; its steroid arm is treated as one "mixed" agent cluster and compared with the whole control arm, and its sub-arms are not compared against a shared control.
*(4) REMAP-CAP contrast (decision D2a, 30 October 2026; extended to the broader short-term sets on 10 November 2026).* The protocol specified that REMAP-CAP enters through an adjusted contrast but did not name which of its contrasts. In the short-term analyses (F1, F3, F4) REMAP-CAP enters through its adjusted ICU-discharge contrast (log-OR 0.399, SE 0.487), the earliest adjusted mortality contrast it reports; its day-90 pooled contrast (log-OR 0.445, SE 0.362) is used only in the 90-day analysis. Both standard errors are derived from the published 95% credible intervals and were not supplied by the investigators; they are frozen approximations, with sensitivity fits at SE 0.40 and 0.60 (ICU discharge) and 0.30 and 0.45 (day 90). This was decided on 30 October, after a first run on 29 October that used the day-90 contrast in the primary short-term fit; that earlier specification, and the exclusion of REMAP-CAP, are reported as sensitivity analyses.
*(5) Sensitivity fits (vi) and (viii), added 16 October 2026 with protocol v2 (decisions A and B).* Fit (vi) excludes trials whose data come from a published subgroup of a larger trial (APROCCHSS in the primary set; also Tongyoo in the broader sets), and fit (viii) excludes the trial that adds a mineralocorticoid (APROCCHSS, hydrocortisone plus fludrocortisone). In the primary set the two fits are identical because they remove the same trial.
*(6) Analyses added in or after the first run (29 October 2026), signed off by the lead on 10 November 2026.* A common-effect fit (the between-trial SD forced near zero, with the exact binomial likelihood; run for F1, F3 and F4) was added on 29 October after reading the supplement of Pitre et al. 2025, whose Bayesian model is common-effect. Also added: a cross-check using the published individual-patient-data rows of Smit 2025 (rank 2 of the data-source hierarchy fixed on 16 October; never merged with the rank-1 row), a source check for the Meijvis 2011 placebo death count (11 in the primary report, 9 in two IPD analyses; settled on 30 October with the primary report kept in the main fits), alternative-denominator checks for ESCAPe, Confalonieri and Nafae (the Nafae placebo denominator of 19 was kept, 20 shown as a check), a pooled-baseline comparison model, and the coding of the timing covariate. On 10 November 2026, after the first results, a frequentist REML (Hartung–Knapp) cross-check and fits with the ICU-discharge standard error set to 0.40 and 0.60 were added. All are sensitivity or cross-check analyses; none was used to choose the primary specification.
*(7) Software (documented deviation, recorded 29 October 2026).* The protocol names brms. The model is fitted in Stan through cmdstanr because brms cannot combine a binomial and a contrast likelihood under one shared effect. brms is used only as a cross-check of the pure-count and pure-contrast cases; the two agree on all 53 compared parameters (checked on 10 November 2026 with 40,000 draws on each side).
*(8) Absolute effects (deviation from protocol section 7.3, first run 29 October 2026, recorded 10 November 2026).* The protocol specified a baseline severe-CAP mortality of 10 to 12%, with sensitivity at 6% and 18%. The analysis reports the absolute risk reduction and number needed to treat at control risks of 10%, 20% and 30% and at the pooled control risk of the trials in each fit (19.8% in F1, 17.4% in F4). The grid was set when the pipeline was built and was not a separate decision.
*(9) Threshold for clinically meaningful benefit (deviation from protocol section 7.3, first run 29 October 2026, recorded 10 November 2026).* The analysis reports P(OR < 0.90), as in the protocol, and P(OR < 0.833), which is 1/1.2 and the threshold of REMAP-CAP's futility rule, instead of the protocol's P(OR < 0.82). The tipping-point criteria are as in the protocol (median OR crossing 0.90; P(OR < 1) below 0.975).
*(10) Sampling and convergence rule (first run 29 October 2026, recorded 10 November 2026).* The protocol's brms settings were 4 chains, 4,000 iterations including 1,000 warm-up, seed 1 and effective sample size above 1,000. The Stan fits use 4 chains with 1,000 warm-up and 2,000 post-warmup draws each (8,000 draws), seed 7291, adapt_delta 0.99 (raised automatically to 0.999 and 0.9999 if there are divergences) and max_treedepth 12; a fit is accepted if R-hat is below 1.01, effective sample size above 400 and there are no divergences. All 56 main fits pass, and the lowest effective sample size (1,456) exceeds the protocol's 1,000.
